Jiayi Lin

Email: jiayi.lin@qmul.ac.uk | [CV]



Jiayi Lin is a final-year Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London, supervised by Prof. Shaogang Gong. She received her master's degree at School of Artificial Intelligence, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2021, supervised by Prof. Liang Wang. And in 2018, she received her bachelor's degree from Sun Yat-sen University.

Research Directions: Computer Vision (Object Detection and Segmentation), Neuro-Symbolic Learning, Deep Learning.
